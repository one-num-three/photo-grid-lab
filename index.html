WHERE THE IDEA STARTED

An album, first.
A camera, second.

The grid grew out of an album-first camera concept: choose where a photo belongs before you take it. These are visual concepts from that earlier study.

Visual studies, not implemented app screens. The working prototype above focuses on the photo grid.
