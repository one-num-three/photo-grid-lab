# Third-party materials

## Sample photographs

The 28 sample photographs in `demo/photos/`, their embedded copies in `demo/index.html`, and their appearances in preview images, the GIF and the MP4 come from Unsplash. Source image identifiers and URLs are listed in [demo/photo-sources.json](demo/photo-sources.json).

They remain subject to the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), rather than the project's MIT code license. The photographs are mock content for this interaction demo; the repository is not a stock-photo collection or photo distribution service.

## Design concepts and fonts

The album-camera concepts in `assets/album-*.webp` were generated for the earlier app design study. They are included as visual references. They are not screenshots of implemented app features.

The site uses the viewer's system fonts and does not distribute SF Pro, PingFang or another proprietary font file. The project mark is original SVG artwork.

Apple Photos inspired the interaction direction. No Apple source code, private API, logo or product assets are included. Apple and Apple Photos are names of their respective owners; this is an independent project.
